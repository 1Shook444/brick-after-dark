How to play

Swipe the scene or tap the buttons. Keyboard: ←/→ or A/D to dodge, ↑/W/Space to jump, ↓/S to slide. P/Esc opens Pause & Shop. Resume continues the same run.

Keep changing lanes: monster pairs aim late and lunge into your path. Some rows block all three lanes — you must jump the low wall/rubble/fence/coffin or slide under the gate/pipe/sign in the traversable lane. Vans, tall crates and monsters require dodging. There is a reachable route, not always an empty lane. Each pickup adds 25 score points and earns 1 spendable brick (2 while the 30-second Double bricks power is active). Distance adds score, never currency. Run earnings enter your wallet once when the run ends.

Start at 22 m/s with waves every 24m. Speed rises by 2 m/s after each randomly chosen 200–300m interval, up to 36 m/s. Switching tabs pauses the run and all power timers. Pause-shop purchases use only banked bricks and activate on Resume; post-run purchases activate next run. One power slot per run. Your wallet, equipped power, best score and name are saved in this browser. Each finished run submits its score once.
